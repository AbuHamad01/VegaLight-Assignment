Task 3: Map of Prices Across Dubai's Communities

Question: Which community has the largest number of sales of units and villas, and how many sales is it? Is this visualization effective in finding this community? Why?
Answer: Al Barsha South Fourth has the largest number of unit/villa sales, with 3,925 sales. This visualization is not very effective for finding the community with the most sales because the map color represents median price per square metre, not sales volume. The sales count is available through the tooltip, so finding the maximum requires checking communities individually.
